A few practical questions

Do I need to be a manager?

No. Coaching is for developers and technical professionals at any career stage. You can work on an immediate situation or prepare for more responsibility.

Can my company pay for individual coaching?

Yes. Companies can arrange one-to-one coaching for an employee, a team workshop, or a combination. We discuss your needs before choosing a format.

What does interview preparation cover?

We focus on how you communicate: introducing yourself, explaining your experience, answering questions, and asking your own. This is interview communication practice rather than coding-test preparation.

Can we work together in person?

Yes. Individual coaching and company workshops are available online or in person by arrangement. For in-person sessions, travel and any accommodation costs are covered by the client, in addition to the coaching fee. We agree on the location and all costs before you commit.

Do I need to become more extroverted?

No. We work on practical communication skills that suit your personality, including listening, asking questions, and explaining your ideas.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the format and scope. Tell me what you need, and I’ll share the proposed approach and price before you decide. For in-person coaching, I’ll also outline travel and any accommodation costs for your approval.
